❓ **Q2** — **The retention window reaches an outbound message**: ADR 0011 held that it does *not* reach a moderation action, because a ban is permanent and a trimmed action handle is a ban nothing can lift. Sending has no such claim on the future: an outbound message's reason to exist is the line it draws and the echoes it suppresses, and both of those die with the chat around them at thirty days. Exempting it would be inheriting ADR 0011's exception without inheriting its argument.

Its own clock is ChatTop's — the instant it was typed — which is also what the timeline orders it by, never revised afterwards even when an echo supplies the platform's send time. That keeps one rule per kind of thing in the store, and it keeps the operator's line where they typed it rather than having it jump when YouTube's stamp arrives.

➡️ Yes: trimmed on the same thirty-day window as chat, ordered by the instant it was typed, and that instant is never revised.
