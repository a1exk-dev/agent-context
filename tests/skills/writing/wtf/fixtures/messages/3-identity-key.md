❓ **Q2** — **Is the identity platform-global or channel-scoped?** Within one platform, is the key `(platform, nativeId)` — the same chatter is one participant wherever they're seen — or `(platform, channel, nativeId)`? The reads push the other way on each platform: YouTube's identifiers are all *channel* ids while the credential is account-scoped, and Twitch's Shared Chat means a message in your channel can originate in another broadcaster's channel and carry *that* channel's badges, so "the channel this message belongs to" is already two fields. At 1.0.0 there's one owned channel per platform, so the distinction only bites in storage shape and in Shared Chat.

➡️ Yes to platform-global: the key is `(platform, nativeId)`. The channel is recorded on the **message** (both the watched channel and, on Twitch, the source channel), never inside the participant's identity. This keeps the map's "schema still records the channel" promise without making a chatter two participants.
